## Decisions

- **AppSidebar module shape:** Treat `AppSidebar` as a self-contained folder under `navigation/` with `_constants` / `_types` / `_utils` / `_hooks` / `_components`, analogous to route-refactoring standards, with `app-sidebar.tsx` as the composition entry (not `page.tsx`, which is reserved for `app/` routes).

## Results
